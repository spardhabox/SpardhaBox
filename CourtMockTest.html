Section 1: Marathi Language Q. 1–10 · 10 Marks
Q. 01  ·  Marathi
खालीलपैकी शुद्ध शब्द ओळखा. / Identify the correctly spelled word:
'सौजन्यशील' is the correct spelling. The word combines सौजन्य + शील. Option A and C look the same — C is the accepted standard form with the correct vowel marks.
Q. 02  ·  Marathi
'निष्काळजी' या शब्दाचा विरुद्धार्थी शब्द ओळखा. / Find the antonym of 'निष्काळजी':
'निष्काळजी' means careless/negligent. Its antonym is 'सावध' (careful/alert/cautious).
Q. 03  ·  Marathi
'डोळ्यांत तेल घालून बसणे' या वाक्प्रचाराचा अर्थ सांगा. / Meaning of this idiom:
'डोळ्यांत तेल घालून बसणे' means to be extremely watchful and vigilant — to guard something very carefully without sleeping.
Q. 04  ·  Marathi
'सुनीता उद्या पुण्यास जाईल.' या वाक्याचा काळ ओळखा. / Identify the tense:
'जाईल' is a simple future tense verb (साधा भविष्यकाळ). The word 'उद्या' (tomorrow) confirms it is a future action.
Q. 05  ·  Marathi
'सज्जन' या शब्दातील संधी ओळखा. / Identify the type of sandhi in 'सज्जन':
'सज्जन' = सत् + जन. When consonant 'त्' meets 'ज', it becomes 'ज्ज' — this is व्यंजनसंधी (consonant sandhi).
Q. 06  ·  Marathi
'जो सर्व ठिकाणी असतो' या शब्दसमूहासाठी योग्य एक शब्द कोणता? / One word substitute:
'सर्वव्यापी' means omnipresent — present everywhere. सर्वज्ञ = omniscient (knows everything), सर्वशक्तिमान = omnipotent.
Q. 07  ·  Marathi
'मुलाने आईला पत्र लिहिले.' या वाक्यातील 'आईला' शब्दाची विभक्ती ओळखा. / Identify the vibhakti of 'आईला':
'आईला' uses the suffix 'ला' which is the marker of चतुर्थी (4th case / dative) — indicating the indirect object — the one to whom the letter was written.
Q. 08  ·  Marathi
'आज पाऊस पडला आणि थंडी वाढली.' या वाक्याचा प्रकार ओळखा. / Identify the sentence type:
Two independent clauses joined by 'आणि' (and) = संयुक्त वाक्य (compound sentence). Both clauses can stand alone.
Q. 09  ·  Marathi
'विद्वान' या शब्दाचे स्त्रीलिंगी रूप काय? / Feminine form of 'विद्वान'?
The Sanskrit-derived feminine of 'विद्वान्' is 'विदुषी'. This is a special irregular gender form from Sanskrit grammar.
Q. 10  ·  Marathi
'माझे जीवन गाणे' हे काव्य कोणाचे आहे? / 'माझे जीवन गाणे' is a poem by:
'माझे जीवन गाणे' is a celebrated poem by G. D. Madgulkar (ग. दि. माडगूळकर), the legendary Marathi lyricist and poet.
Section 2: English Language Q. 11–30 · 20 Marks
Directions (Q. 11–15): Fill in the blanks (A–E) in the passage below with the most appropriate word from the options given.

The Indian judiciary plays a vital role in (A) _______ the rights of citizens. From district courts to the Supreme Court, the system is (B) _______ to deliver justice to all. Judges are (C) _______ by the President of India in consultation with the collegium. The High Courts have (D) _______ jurisdiction over civil and criminal matters within their respective states. It is essential that citizens (E) _______ themselves with their legal rights to seek timely justice.
Q. 11  ·  English — Passage Fill
Which word best fits blank (A)?
The judiciary's role is to safeguard (protect) citizens' rights — the sentence requires a positive action verb in this context.
Q. 12  ·  English — Passage Fill
Which word best fits blank (B)?
The judicial system is 'designed' (structured/built) to deliver justice — this is the correct colocation.
Q. 13  ·  English — Passage Fill
Which word best fits blank (C)?
Judges in India are 'appointed' by the President — they are not elected. This is a factual GK + vocabulary question.
Q. 14  ·  English — Passage Fill
Which word best fits blank (D)?
High Courts exercise both original jurisdiction (trial) and appellate jurisdiction (hearing appeals from lower courts).
Q. 15  ·  English — Passage Fill
Which word best fits blank (E)?
'Acquaint themselves with' means to become familiar with/get to know — the correct phrasal collocation here.
Q. 16  ·  English — Spelling
Find the correctly spelt word:
C-O-M-M-I-T-T-E-E: double 'm', double 't', double 'e' at the end. This is a very commonly misspelled word.
Q. 17  ·  English — Spelling
Find the correctly spelt word:
H-A-R-A-S-S-M-E-N-T: single 'r', double 's'. No extra 'e' after the double 's'.
Q. 18  ·  English — Vocabulary
Choose the synonym of 'Tenacious':
'Tenacious' means holding firmly, not giving up easily. Its synonym is 'Persistent' — continuing despite obstacles.
Q. 19  ·  English — Vocabulary
Choose the antonym of 'Tranquil':
'Tranquil' = calm and peaceful. Its antonym is 'Turbulent' = agitated, disturbed, not calm.
Q. 20  ·  English — Preposition
Fill in the blank with correct preposition: 'The accused was charged _____ theft.'
'Charged with' is the correct legal collocation. 'Charged with theft/murder' is standard legal English.
Q. 21  ·  English — Error Spotting
Find the error: 'Neither the manager / nor the employees / was present / at the meeting.'
With 'Neither...nor', the verb agrees with the noun closest to it ('employees' is plural). So it should be 'were present', not 'was present'. Error is in Part C.
Q. 22  ·  English — Voice
Change the Voice: 'The clerk typed the letter.'
Simple Past Active → Simple Past Passive: Subject + was/were + past participle + by + agent. 'The letter was typed by the clerk.'
Q. 23  ·  English — Narration
Change the Narration: She said to me, "Please help me with this file."
A polite request in direct speech becomes 'requested + object + to + infinitive' in reported speech. 'Please help me' → 'requested me to help her'.
Q. 24  ·  English — Question Tag
Supply the correct Question Tag: 'He has never visited Delhi, _____?'
'Never' is a negative word, so the sentence is already negative in meaning. For negative sentences, use an affirmative question tag: 'has he'. The auxiliary is 'has' (present perfect).
Q. 25  ·  English — Idiom
What is the meaning of the idiom 'Bite the bullet'?
'Bite the bullet' originates from the practice of biting a bullet during surgery to cope with pain. Now it means to endure something painful or difficult stoically.
Q. 26  ·  English — Article
Fill with correct Article: 'She is _____ M.A. in Economics.'
'M.A.' is pronounced as "em-ay" — it starts with a vowel sound ('em'). So we use 'an' before it. Rule: use 'an' before vowel sounds, not just vowel letters.
Q. 27  ·  English — Word Forms
What is the noun form of the adjective 'Diligent'?
Diligent (adj) → Diligence (noun). Option A is an adverb, C and D are not real words.
Q. 28  ·  English — Tense
Fill in: 'By the time the inspector arrives, the culprit _____ escaped.'
'By the time + present simple' signals a Future Perfect: 'will have escaped'. The action (escaping) will be completed before a future point (inspector's arrival).
Q. 29  ·  English — One Word
Choose the word which means 'a place where public records are kept':
Archive = a place where historical documents and public records are stored. Apiary = bee farm, Abattoir = slaughterhouse, Amphitheatre = open-air performance venue.
Q. 30  ·  English — Plurals
What is the plural form of 'Datum'?
Latin plurals: Datum → Data, Criterion → Criteria, Syllabus → Syllabi. 'Data' is technically plural, though often used as singular in everyday English.
Section 3: General Knowledge Q. 31–40 · 10 Marks
Q. 31  ·  General Knowledge — Constitution
Which Article of the Indian Constitution deals with the 'Right to Constitutional Remedies'? / 'घटनात्मक उपायांचा अधिकार' कोणत्या अनुच्छेदात आहे?
Article 32 provides the Right to Constitutional Remedies. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar called it the 'heart and soul of the Constitution'. It allows citizens to approach the Supreme Court to enforce Fundamental Rights.

Koyna Hydroelectric Project is located in which district of Maharashtra? / कोयना जलविद्युत प्रकल्प कोणत्या जिल्ह्यात आहे?
The Koyna Hydroelectric Project is located in Satara district, Maharashtra. It is India's largest completed hydroelectric power plant, on the Koyna River.

The 58th Jnanpith Award was jointly awarded to Gulzar (Urdu/Hindi) and Jagadguru Rambhadracharya (Sanskrit). Both are correct — but among the options, Jagadguru Rambhadracharya is listed as C, which is an accepted answer. Gulzar (A) is also a co-recipient.
Q. 34  ·  GK — Current Appointment ★
Who was appointed as the 51st Chief Justice of India in November 2024? / नोव्हेंबर २०२४ मध्ये ५१वे सरन्यायाधीश म्हणून कोणाची नियुक्ती?

Q. 35  ·  GK — Sports ★

India won 6 medals at the Paris Olympics 2024: 1 Silver (Neeraj Chopra, Javelin) + 5 Bronze (Manu Bhaker ×2, Swapnil Kusale, Hockey team, Aman Sehrawat). Ranked 71st overall.
Q. 36  ·  GK — Maharashtra History
Who was the first woman Chief Minister of Maharashtra? / महाराष्ट्राच्या पहिल्या महिला मुख्यमंत्री कोण?
Maharashtra has never had a female Chief Minister as of 2025. This is an important GK fact for state-level exams.
Q. 37  ·  GK — Judiciary
The Bombay High Court was established in which year? / मुंबई उच्च न्यायालयाची स्थापना कोणत्या वर्षी झाली?
The Bombay High Court was established on 14 August 1862 under the High Courts Act, 1861 — along with the Calcutta and Madras High Courts.
Q. 38  ·  GK — Science
What is the chemical formula of common salt (Table Salt)? / सामान्य मिठाचे रासायनिक सूत्र?

26 November = Constitution Day (Samvidhan Diwas). The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on this day in 1949. It came into effect on 26 January 1950 (Republic Day).

Sagareshwar Wildlife Sanctuary is located in Sangli district, Maharashtra. It is a small protected area near Sagareshwar, known for its deer population.
Section 4: General Intelligence & Reasoning Q. 41–60 · 20 Marks
Q. 41 · Reasoning — Series
Complete the series: 5, 11, 23, 47, 95, ?
Pattern: each term = 2×previous + 1. So 95×2+1 = 191.
Q. 42 · Reasoning — Letter-Number Series
Find the missing term: B2Z, D4X, F6V, H8T, ?
Letters+2: B→D→F→H→J. Numbers+2: 2→4→6→8→10. Reverse letters-2: Z→X→V→T→R. Answer: J10R.
Q. 43 · Reasoning — Coding
If JUDGE is coded as 10-21-4-7-5, how will COURT be coded?
Alphabetical position coding: C=3, O=15, U=21, R=18, T=20 → 3-15-21-18-20.
Q. 44 · Reasoning — Blood Relations
Pointing to a photograph, a man said, "She is the daughter of the only son of my grandfather." How is the woman related to the man? / एका फोटोकडे बोट दाखवत एक माणूस म्हणाला, "ती माझ्या आजोबांच्या एकुलत्या एका मुलाची मुलगी आहे."
Grandfather's only son = the man's father. The woman is that son's daughter = the man's sister.
Q. 45 · Reasoning — Distance
Sita walks 6 km East, then turns South and walks 8 km. What is the shortest distance from her starting point? / सीता 6 किमी पूर्वेला चालते, मग दक्षिणेकडे 8 किमी चालते. सुरुवातीपासून किमान अंतर किती?
Using Pythagoras: √(6² + 8²) = √(36+64) = √100 = 10 km. Classic 6-8-10 right triangle.
Q. 46 · Reasoning — Syllogism
Statements: All lawyers are judges. Some judges are clerks.
Conclusion 1: Some clerks are lawyers. Conclusion 2: All judges are lawyers.
/ सर्व वकील न्यायाधीश आहेत. काही न्यायाधीश लिपिक आहेत.
We cannot reverse 'All lawyers are judges' to 'All judges are lawyers' — Conclusion 2 is wrong. 'Some judges are clerks' but we don't know which judges, so Conclusion 1 also doesn't follow definitively. Neither follows.
Q. 47 · Reasoning — Odd One Out
Find the odd one out: 8, 27, 64, 100, 125
8=2³, 27=3³, 64=4³, 125=5³ are all perfect cubes. 100 = 10² is a perfect square, NOT a perfect cube. So 100 is the odd one out.
Q. 48 · Reasoning — Clock
What is the angle between the hands of a clock at exactly 3:15? / बरोबर 3:15 वाजता घड्याळाच्या काट्यांमधील कोन?
Formula: |30H − 5.5M| = |30×3 − 5.5×15| = |90 − 82.5| = 7.5°. The hour hand moves 0.5° per minute.
Q. 49 · Reasoning — Calendar
If 1st January 2023 was a Sunday, what day was 1st January 2024? / 1 जानेवारी 2023 रोजी रविवार असेल, तर 1 जानेवारी 2024 रोजी कोणता वार?
2023 is a non-leap year (365 days = 52 weeks + 1 odd day). Sunday + 1 = Monday.
Q. 50 · Reasoning — Ranking
In a row of 50 students, Rekha is 18th from the front and Seema is 22nd from the back. How many students are between them? / 50 विद्यार्थ्यांच्या रांगेत रेखा पुढून 18वी, सीमा मागून 22वी. मध्ये किती?
Seema's position from front = 50−22+1 = 29. Between Rekha (18) and Seema (29): 29−18−1 = 10.

Q. 59 · Reasoning — Blood Relations
A man said to a woman, "Your only brother's son is my wife's brother." How is the woman related to the man's wife? / एक पुरुष महिलेला म्हणाला, "तुझ्या एकुलत्या भावाचा मुलगा माझ्या पत्नीचा भाऊ आहे."
The woman's brother's son = the man's wife's brother. So the woman's brother is the father of the wife's brother = the wife's uncle. The woman is the wife's brother's father's sister = the wife's aunt (मावशी).

Q. 61 · Arithmetic
What is 15% of 25% of 1600? / 1600 च्या 25% च्या 15% किती?
25% of 1600 = 400. 15% of 400 = 60.
Q. 62 · Arithmetic
If A's income is 20% less than B's, by what % is B's income more than A's? / A चे उत्पन्न B पेक्षा 20% कमी असेल तर B चे A पेक्षा किती % जास्त?
Let B=100, A=80. B more than A = 20/80 × 100 = 25%.
Q. 63 · Arithmetic
A shopkeeper buys an article for Rs.450, marks it 40% above cost, gives 10% discount. Find selling price.
Marked price = 450 × 1.4 = 630. After 10% discount: 630 × 0.9 = 567.
Q. 64 · Arithmetic
Average of 9 numbers is 40. If one number is replaced: 20 becomes 60. What is new average? / 9 संख्यांची सरासरी 40 आहे. एक संख्या 20 ऐवजी 60 केल्यास नवी सरासरी?
New sum = 9×40 − 20 + 60 = 360 + 40 = 400. New average = 400/9 ≈ 44.44.
Q. 65 · Arithmetic
P does work in 12 days, Q in 18 days. They work together 4 days; P leaves. How many more days for Q? / P 12 दिवसांत, Q 18 दिवसांत काम करतो. 4 दिवस एकत्र केल्यावर P निघतो. Q ला उर्वरित काम किती दिवसांत?
Combined rate = 1/12+1/18 = 5/36. In 4 days: 20/36 = 5/9 done. Remaining = 4/9. Q's time = (4/9)÷(1/18) = (4/9)×18 = 8 days.
Q. 66 · Arithmetic
A 200m long train passes a pole in 10 seconds. What is its speed in km/hr? / 200 मीटर लांबीची ट्रेन खांबापाशी 10 सेकंदांत जाते. वेग किमी/तास मध्ये?
Speed = 200÷10 = 20 m/s = 20 × 18/5 = 72 km/hr.
Q. 67 · Arithmetic
Rs.1800 divided among X, Y, Z in ratio 3:4:5. Find Z's share. / 1800 रुपये 3:4:5 प्रमाणात वाटले. Z चा वाटा किती?
Total parts = 12. Z = 5 parts = 5/12 × 1800 = Rs. 750.
Q. 68 · Arithmetic
Find Compound Interest on Rs.8000 at 10% p.a. for 2 years. / 8000 रुपयांवर 10% दराने 2 वर्षांसाठी चक्रवाढ व्याज किती?
CI = P[(1+r/100)² − 1] = 8000 × [1.21 − 1] = 8000 × 0.21 = Rs. 1680.
Q. 69 · Arithmetic
A cone has base radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find its slant height. / शंकूची त्रिज्या 7 सेमी, उंची 24 सेमी. तिरकस उंची किती?
l = √(r² + h²) = √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25 cm. Classic 7-24-25 Pythagorean triple.
Q. 70 · Arithmetic
60 litres mixture has milk:water = 5:1. How much water to add to make it 5:3? / 60 लिटर मिश्रणात दूध:पाणी = 5:1. 5:3 करण्यासाठी किती पाणी?
Initial: milk=50L, water=10L. Let x be water added. 50/(10+x)=5/3 → 150=50+5x → x=20 litres.
Q. 71 · Arithmetic
Ratio of ages of A:B = 5:6. Five years ago ratio was 4:5. Find A's present age.
Let ages = 5k, 6k. (5k-5)/(6k-5)=4/5 → 25k-25=24k-20 → k=5. A = 5×5 = 25 years.
Q. 72 · Arithmetic
Which is the smallest fraction? / सर्वात लहान अपूर्णांक कोणता?
Convert to decimals: 5/7≈0.714, 4/9≈0.444, 7/11≈0.636, 3/5=0.6. Smallest = 4/9 ≈ 0.444.
Q. 73 · Arithmetic
If x − 1/x = 3, find x² + 1/x². / जर x − 1/x = 3 असेल, तर x² + 1/x² ची किंमत काढा.
(x−1/x)² = x² − 2 + 1/x² = 9. Therefore x² + 1/x² = 9 + 2 = 11.
Q. 74 · Arithmetic
Two dice thrown simultaneously. Probability of getting sum = 7? / दोन फासे एकाच वेळी फेकल्यास बेरीज 7 येण्याची संभाव्यता?
